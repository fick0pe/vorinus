RESOURCE PACK COMBINER

Upload several .zip resource packs, drag them into priority order, then merge. Files from packs higher in the list overwrite matching files from packs below — same rule Minecraft uses when you stack packs in game. Priority order is remembered per pack, even after a pack is updated to a new version. Duplicate packs and non-pack zips are flagged automatically, and you can export/import your whole setup as a JSON file.
